## Contributions:

All team members initially worked on their own implementation of autonomous driving that fuses lidar and camera. 

Theodoros and Farbod's implementations performed the best on the first attempt, so the group decided to adopt them as the base and everyone kept improving them from there. Thomas improved on Theo's progress and Jack improved on Farbod's progress. 

Theo wrote the gap-following algorithm along with the perception pipeline, including depth.py, fusion.py and masks.py;
All team members were throughly involved in testing on the track and the tuning of launch parameters;

Thomas improved gap-following and speed control using a simulator script for testing safety, lap-counting, and navigating obstacles, which helped in tuning launch paramters;

Chirag fixed the car's tendency to drive close to the inner wall and increased the lowest speed near obstacles;

To document progress, Farbod kept track of the lap time and Thomas recorded videos;

Jack, Thomas, and Theo worked on docstrings, code comments and README.
